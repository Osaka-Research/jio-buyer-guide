What Is the Secondary Market?

Once Jio lists on Oct 28, 2026, its shares trade publicly on NSE and BSE just like any other stock — Reliance, TCS, Infosys. You missed the IPO? No problem. You can buy from other sellers at the live market price. This is called the secondary market.

You don't need to apply for anything. You just need a demat + trading account and money.

What You Need Before Oct 28

Demat Account — where shares are stored electronically. Open one on Zerodha, Groww, or Upstox (free, takes 10 min with Aadhaar).
Trading Account — linked to your demat. Usually opened together. Needed to place buy/sell orders.
KYC Complete — Aadhaar + PAN verified. Must be done before you can trade.
Funds Added — add money to your trading account via UPI or bank transfer before market opens.

How to Buy on Listing Day (Oct 28)

⏰ Pre-open session: 9:00–9:35 AM — place limit orders only. Price discovery happens here. Trading opens at 10:00 AM.

📱 Zerodha (Kite)

  1. Open Kite app → search JPL
  2. Tap the stock → tap BUY
  3. Choose order type:
    Market / Limit / SL
  4. Enter quantity
  5. Review → Place Order
  6. Shares credited by 5 PM

📱 Groww

  1. Open Groww → Stocks tab
  2. Search Jio Platforms
  3. Tap Buy
  4. Enter shares + order type
  5. Approve UPI payment request
  6. Shares in demat same day

When to Buy — 3 Strategies

Strategy 1 — Listing Day (High Risk / High Reward)

GMP suggests listing around ₹1,379. If you buy at open, you're paying a 23% premium over IPO price. Works if the stock keeps rising. Risky if it corrects after the first hour.

📌 Tip: Watch the first 30 min. If it holds above ₹1,300, momentum is strong. If it dips below ₹1,200, wait.

Strategy 2 — Wait 1–2 Weeks (Medium Risk)

Most IPOs see a correction 5–10 days after listing as IPO allottees book profits. Wait for the sell-off, then buy the dip at a better price.

📌 Target entry: ₹1,100–1,200 range if correction happens.

Strategy 3 — SIP / Monthly Accumulate (Low Risk)

Don't try to time it. Buy a fixed amount every month (₹5,000–₹10,000). You average out the price over time. Best for long-term holders.
